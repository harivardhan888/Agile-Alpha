SIMONA JANKOWSKI:
Thank you. Good afternoon, everyone, and welcome to NVIDIA's third-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings call. Joining me today are Jensen Huang, President and Chief Executive Officer; and Colette Kress, Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer. 

Before we begin, I'd like to remind you that our remarks today contain forward-looking statements that are subject to risks and uncertainties.

COLETTE KRESS:
Thank you, Simona. Q3 was an exceptional quarter. Total revenue was a record $30.0 billion, up 15% sequentially and up 85% year-over-year. This staggering growth was driven primarily by our Data Center platform, which generated $25.5 billion in revenue, up 110% from the same quarter last year. 

Gross Margin for the quarter came in at 75.1%, expanding by 150 basis points from last quarter. This margin expansion is largely due to the favorable mix shift toward our high-end Blackwell architectures and robust software revenue from NVIDIA AI Enterprise. 

Operating expenses were $3.8 billion, up 12% sequentially, reflecting aggressive investments in R&D for custom silicon and next-generation robotics platforms. Net income for the quarter was $16.9 billion, translating to earnings per share of $6.80.

Looking at our balance sheet, we generated $12.5 billion in free cash flow this quarter. We ended the quarter with $45 billion in cash and marketable securities. Our debt-to-equity ratio remains incredibly healthy at 0.15x.

JENSEN HUANG:
Thank you, Colette. We are witnessing an industrial revolution. Every country, every major enterprise, and every startup is racing to build generative AI capabilities. 

Our new Blackwell architecture is now in full production. The demand is completely off the charts. We have successfully deployed Blackwell clusters to AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, and Oracle Cloud. The performance leap is massive—Blackwell is delivering 5x the inference performance of Hopper while consuming half the energy per token.

We are also seeing incredible momentum in Edge AI. Demand for our Jetson platforms has increased by 40% year-over-year, as automotive and heavy manufacturing industries deploy autonomous robots.

Looking to Q4, we expect total revenue to be $32.5 billion, plus or minus 2%. We anticipate our gross margins to remain stable at around 75%. We are fundamentally constrained by supply, not demand. We are working closely with TSMC and our supply chain partners to increase capacity through 2027.

The future of computing is accelerated, and the future of software is AI.
